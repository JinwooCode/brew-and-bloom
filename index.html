How it gets to you

No warehouses, no middlemen, no beans sitting on a shelf for six months.

Sourced directly

We buy from a handful of farms and co-ops we visit each season, and we pay above the fair-trade floor.

Roasted in small batches

Twelve kilos at a time on a 1962 drum roaster, profiled by hand and cupped before a single bag is filled.

Shipped within 24 hours

Bagged with a one-way valve and posted the same morning, so it reaches your grinder at peak freshness.

Fresh beans, every two weeks

Pick a roast, pick a grind, and we send 250g every other Tuesday. Skip, swap or cancel any time — no lock-in.
